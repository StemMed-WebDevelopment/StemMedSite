Introducing STEM-med Stories

By The STEM-med Team · October 28, 2026

Behind every Narcan kit handed out and every training session held, there are people: volunteers giving their time, community leaders who've been doing this work for years, and neighbors whose lives have been touched by the opioid crisis. STEM-med Stories is where we'll share them.

What you'll read here

  • Community leader interviews: conversations with the people working on public health in Vermilion County.
  • Team updates: what our student officers and volunteers are working on, and what we're learning along the way.
  • Our progress: as we begin training and distribution, we'll share honest updates on where things stand.

Share your story

Do you know someone making a difference in our community, or have a story you think people should hear? We'd love to talk with you. Email us at stemmedproject@gmail.com.

And if you love writing, we're looking for a Journalist to lead this newsletter. See our open officer positions.
